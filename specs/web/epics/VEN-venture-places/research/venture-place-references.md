## The founder's top three compared [judgment unless marked]
- **What they share:** all three lead with the place, not the model, and repeat one dominant booking action everywhere [verified]. None of the pages read uses investment language [verified]. What she likes is hospitality design, not investor design.
- **ourhabitas.com (rank 1):** the most cinematic and brand-led. It has multi-property navigation plus films, journal and impact sections [verified],\[37\] and is a custom build. Mood is its strength.
- **outpost-x.com (rank 2):** dense and playful, like a product catalogue. It lists ten bookable units with from-prices, Stripe day passes and a mailing-list pop-up [verified],\[12\] and is a template build. It is the fastest route from interest to booking, but its sci-fi tone is the furthest from a historic Italian property.
- **mandali.org (rank 3):** the closest in subject: an Italian retreat centre with a founders' letter, teachers and dated programmes [verified].\[15\] It has a video banner and is a designer build. Its weakness is stale dates [verified].
- **Synthesis:** she ranks mood above fit. The gallery should let her pick mood separately from structure, and the spectrums do that.

## Proposed venture-place spectrums [PROPOSED]
None of these re-asks ground, stillness or density, and none duplicates the feel forks. All are structure forks.

1. **Place first ↔ Model first**
   - *Place first means:* the home page opens on the property and life there, and the model sits behind a request.
   - *Model first means:* how ownership works, the phases and the deck request appear above the fold.
   - Sites: place first is ourhabitas.com, mandali.org and ecovillagefindhorn.uk; model first is wuweivillage.com/invest and neos.life/village.
   - *Pass:* the two ends produce different home pages, and the choice depends on which audience leads and what counsel allows, which a designer cannot overrule. If counsel rules out model first, the question is withdrawn.
2. **As it is ↔ As it will be**
   - *As it is means:* current photographs lead, including unrestored rooms, and renders sit in a labelled plans section.
   - *As it will be means:* labelled renders lead, with current photos shown as evidence of today.
   - Sites: as it is is torri-superiore.org and sextantio.it; as it will be is neos.life/village (its share image is a village render [verified]), with holisticaproperties.com as the unlabelled failure mode.\[7\]
   - *Pass:* it changes the visible lead image and the labelling, and depends on her assets and how comfortable she is showing restoration.
3. **Programme-led ↔ Place-led**
   - *Programme-led means:* the home page is organised around dated retreats, residencies and the summit, with a calendar near the top.
   - *Place-led means:* the home page is organised around rooms, spaces and land, and programmes sit on their own page.
   - Sites: programme-led is mandali.org and ecoversity.org/nynesummit; place-led is outpost-x.com, ecovillagefindhorn.uk and sextantio.it.
   - *Pass:* it changes the navigation and home sections, and depends on how often she will run programmes, which only she knows.
4. **Founder's voice ↔ Community's voice**
   - *Founder's voice means:* a founder portrait and first-person letter on the home page.
   - *Community's voice means:* "we" throughout, with governance and members shown and no lead portrait.
   - Sites: founder's voice is holisticaproperties.com/about-us, mandali.org and neos.life/village; community's voice is ecovillagefindhorn.uk and ecovillageithaca.org.
   - *Pass:* a portrait-and-letter block is a different structure from a governance block. It may overlap personVoice. [NEEDS DECISION]
5. **Open door ↔ Members' door**
   - *Open door means:* every page is public, and sign-ups are just forms.
   - *Members' door means:* an account area for members, co-owners and attendees.
   - Sites: open door is mandali.org and ecovillagefindhorn.uk; members' door is traditionaldreamfactory.com [verified].\[38\]
   - *Pass:* it is visibly different (login and account pages) and depends on her governance plans. It feeds supabaseWhoLogsIn before that add-on is bought.

**Rejected:**
- Numbers shown ↔ on request duplicates the ask visibility slot.
- One door ↔ separate doors duplicates howSeparate.
- Quiet ↔ lively collapses into the existing feel forks.

## Proposed style tags [PROPOSED]
- **booking-bar:** a persistent book or rates action in the header (Habitas, Outpost X).
- **date-cards:** dated programme cards (Mandali).
- **stat-counter:** animated counters (Neos). Warning-prone.
- **render-labelled:** a visible label on renders. No site checked was confirmed to do this [NOT FOUND], so the brief should require it.
- **founder-letter:** a first-person founder letter (Mandali, Holistica).
- **legal-footer:** entity details in the footer (Sextantio).\[13\]
