Supplier Profile — Kestrel Provisions Ltd.

Kestrel Provisions Ltd. is a wholesale distributor of restaurant equipment
serving Ontario and Quebec. Founded in 1994, the company operates two warehouses
and employs 140 people.

Kestrel's customers are independent restaurant groups and institutional
kitchens. The company competes primarily on delivery speed and stocks over
11,000 SKUs.

Kestrel is a member of the Canadian Foodservice Distributors Association and
holds ISO 9001 certification.

Head office: 88 Wrenford Road, Mississauga, Ontario.
Enquiries: sales@kestrelprovisions.example
