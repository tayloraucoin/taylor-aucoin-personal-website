Whitlock Joinery — Company Overview

Whitlock Joinery has been building bespoke cabinetry in the Fraser Valley for
eighteen years. We employ eleven people, including four certified journeyman
joiners, and turned over $2.4 million last year.

Our average project value is $38,000 and we complete roughly sixty projects a
year. We hold a WorkSafeBC clearance letter and carry $5 million in liability
coverage.

We were established in 2008 by Marion Whitlock, who trained at the Bruntwood
College of Furniture in England.

We work almost entirely on referral from three architecture practices in
Vancouver.
