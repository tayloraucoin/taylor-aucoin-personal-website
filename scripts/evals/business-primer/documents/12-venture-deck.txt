RIVERSTONE COMMONS
Investor and member briefing — spring

Slide 1 — What this is

Riverstone Commons is a community-owned sanctuary on a 40-hectare hillside estate outside Vellmar. We are converting a nineteenth-century farm and its outbuildings into a place people can live, work, and gather for a season or a lifetime.

Slide 2 — Where we are

We took possession of the estate in March. Restoration of the main house is underway, with the roof finished and the first six rooms habitable by autumn. The barn conversion begins once the main house is watertight.

Slide 3 — The offering

Founding membership: a lifetime right to stay, from €18,000. Twenty founding places, eleven remaining.
Seasonal residencies: eight to twelve weeks, priced per season, applications open in May.
The retreat programme: we intend to run guided retreats in the barn once it is finished.

Slide 4 — Who we are

Dana Whitlow, founder. Dana spent eleven years in regenerative agriculture before starting this and leads land acquisition and the build.
Marek Oyelaran, operations. Marek runs the build programme and the contractor relationships.
Priya Ellsworth, community director. Priya designed the membership model and runs applications.

Slide 5 — The people we're for

Backers who want a stake in a place rather than a fund. Members who want somewhere to return to. Neighbours in Vellmar who should never feel the estate is closed to them.

Slide 6 — What we can and cannot say

Our counsel has asked that we do not describe founding membership as an investment or quote any return. The words "sanctuary" and "community-owned" must appear on every public page. Everything about the raise goes through Dana before it is published.
