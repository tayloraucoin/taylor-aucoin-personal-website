About Marrow & Vane

Marrow & Vane is a two-person brand studio in Halifax, Nova Scotia. We have
been working together under this name since 2016, though we both freelanced
separately for years before that.

We make identities for food and hospitality businesses — restaurants,
small-batch producers, the occasional distillery. Most of our clients are
owner-operators opening their second location, who have realised the logo their
cousin made is not going to carry them any further.

What we are not is an agency. There is no account team and no strategy deck you
will never read again. You work with the two of us directly, start to finish.

People usually come to us because someone they trust sent them. Clients tend to
say the same thing afterwards: that we asked better questions than they
expected, and that the work looked like them rather than like us.

We are members of the Registered Graphic Designers of Nova Scotia. Our work has
been recognised by the Atlantic Design Awards three times, most recently in
2023.

We work across Atlantic Canada, and take on projects further afield when they
are interesting enough to travel for.

If you are thinking about working with us, the best thing to do is send an email
describing the business and where it is stuck. We will tell you honestly whether
we are the right fit.

We are currently booking projects for early next year.
