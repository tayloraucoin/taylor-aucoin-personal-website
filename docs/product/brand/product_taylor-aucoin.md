## The problem, in each audience's terms

**1. Founders, CTOs and heads of product hiring** (primary)

- They "don't have the right person in the room to take their technology product to that next level and really ground things as a system." (Taylor, 14 Oct)
- The questions he brings: "why are we doing this? Who are we serving? What are the behavioral outcomes that they are willing to pay for? The problem space, how does this design work for solving that problem? How does the brand ethos affect the emotion?" (Taylor)
- Why it's sharper now: "you have to think through everything before you do it now." (Taylor)

**2. Founders and businesses buying a website, branding or a build**

- The founder who needs a site "has no idea how any of this AI shit works, um, knows that it's possible to build amazing things and doesn't know where to start." (Taylor)
- Beyond the site itself: "get their branding down packed, which is really important," and how "each sales funnel step needs to play into that." (Taylor)
- For a build: "they need somebody to have some technical direction, uh, whether it be on the product side or the technical side or everything in between." (Taylor)

**3. Learners and peers**

- "there's just a lot of noise about like how to use AI. And it really takes somebody who has just done a lot of reps and has created very clear-sighted systems on how to do this at a high level." (Taylor)
- "I speak in structure… there's a natural teaching to what I do." (Taylor)

## How working with Taylor works

**A role, full-time or contract**

1. "let's book something, we'll talk." (Taylor) The booking link is `cal.com/taylor-aucoin/30min` (`lib/config.ts`).
2. A screening form before the call, proposed by Taylor and not yet built. It asks "what is your proposal? Um, what are you thinking at for a rate, um, type of engagement, et cetera," to keep "bullshit things" out of the calendar.
3. The shape comes from "building around what that circumstance looks like." Rates and boundaries are set case by case, and the boundaries "will get upheld." (Taylor)

**An engineering engagement** (as the site describes it, `content/services.ts`)

1. A 30-minute call, "no pitch."
2. A scoped proposal within 48 hours: scope, timeline, price and start date.
3. A start date. Taylor replies within one business day, and contracts bill monthly.

**A website or branding job**

1. The client fills out the intake form, "and then I just take it from there." (Taylor)
2. "they can have everything ready within a week." (Taylor)
3. The site is built in real code: Next.js, hosted on Vercel. The handover includes a Claude Code guide and an explanation of how their infrastructure works, kept "really simple and gamified for them," so the client has "creative control um, to be able to do whatever they want with it." (Taylor)

**Coaching**

- "I would hear somebody where they're at," then shape the help from there. (Taylor)
