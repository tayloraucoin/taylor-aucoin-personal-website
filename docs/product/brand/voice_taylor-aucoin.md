## Vocabulary

**Use** (from the language bank): end to end · shape the problem · decided · shipped · owned · *you own it* · fixed scope · accountable · judgment · conventions · live.

**Avoid:** leverage · seamless · unlock · empower · transform · journey · cutting-edge · innovative · passionate · "custom" as a selling point · "I do everything" · "AI ninja" (Taylor's own phrase in product.md, but it reads as guru) · "Agora Network Technologies" (never public).

## Rules

1. Sentence case everywhere: headings, buttons, labels.
2. No exclamation marks.
3. First person on the site ("I"); third person only in bios written for others.
4. Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
5. Every claim is backed by a fact from product.md or a named source. No invented metric, logo or quote.
6. Buttons name the consequence: "Book a 30-minute call," never "Submit" or "Learn more."
7. Show capability as decisions: the problem, what was chosen, what happened.
8. AI is described as work under Taylor's judgment ("what I let it do, what I check"), never as speed or magic.
9. Invitation, never pressure: no countdowns, scarcity or urgency.
10. Canadian spelling. [ASSUMPTION]
11. One name per thing. The colours are **Carbon, Gold, Patina, Lamplight**; "verdigris" is retired. Offers keep the names in product.md's terms table.
12. The 3/5 ethos is an undertone: experimentation and learning by doing may show; the term never appears.
