# Taylor Aucoin — Pillar Card

> Brand thread 3 of 3 · Part 1 · 2026-10-14 · Hearth · Confirmed by Taylor
> Method: branding-process.md, Steps 0–5, "running it solo." Paste this card at the top of any brand, design or copy thread.

**Essence:** The whole product, thought through.
**PEM's line:** Systems that produce elegant work. (Also the Structured ∩ Crafted overlap.)
**Purpose (why):** To ground a product as a system and carry it whole, from idea to launch, so the people who own it can keep running it.
**Primary audience:** Founders, CTOs and heads of product at founder-led startups hiring a Product Engineer.
**Main alternatives:** the implementer engineer · candidates with more years on paper · the dev shop that upsells hours · the contractor you have to manage · the site builder people use themselves.

## Pillars (ranked)

1. **Structured** (dominant) — Structured but not rigid.
   Supporting: brilliant systems, mapping everything out from the beginning, blueprint, pattern recognition, conventions.
2. **Crafted** — Crafted but not precious.
   Supporting: design coding hybrid, artist at heart, craftsmanship, builder-maker-designer.
3. **Attuned** — Attuned but not soft.
   Supporting: read the room, a natural teaching, clarity of thought, mind, and heart, guiding.

**When pillars conflict, Structured wins.**

### Each pillar: meaning, a real conflict, and its evidence

**Structured.** The plan, the conventions and the map of the whole come first, and they bend when reality does ("some degree of adaptation… is bound to happen," met with "a clear and calm head").
- *Real conflict:* the family office scope grew mid-engagement. The plan absorbed it rather than refusing it; the CRM was "deploy[ed]… in four days to accommodate an expanded project scope" (Bruno).
- *Audience evidence:* hirers name "what to build, what to gate, what to trust to production" as the scarce asset (Propel Labs). Fractional buyers distrust anyone who "claims to do everything" (fcto.uk); structure is what makes the whole path credible.
- *Proof:* Bruno (conventions, documentation, SOPs), Yogesh ("break down a complex task"), and PEM itself.

**Crafted.** One pair of hands carries the design and the engineering, and the work ships.
- *Real conflict:* Taylor has held back images of his work because his screenshots aren't great ("I've kind of been avoiding that"). Precious says wait for perfect; Crafted shows the real work as it is, including paper design files.
- *Audience evidence:* Linear asks for "Product sensibility: you care about UX, speed, and polish." Propel Labs hires "builder-maker-designers." Lee Robinson: "Great Product Engineers don't stop at visually complete."
- *Proof:* Dawson ("took rough concepts and turned them into full products"); zero reported defects at Roomvy and Calculate.

**Attuned.** Meet each person where they are, and explain until they hold the model themselves, with the boundaries still holding.
- *Real conflict:* the proposed screening form before booking keeps "bullshit things" off the calendar, and "the boundaries… will get upheld." Attuned to the buyer, not available to everyone.
- *Audience evidence:* reactance research (controlling language backfires; invitation works with sceptical buyers). Site buyers are wary and sometimes embarrassed about not being technical. Learners want someone who checks AI's output.
- *Proof:* Bruno ("accessible terms"), Micheal (mentorship), Yogesh ("in-depth explanation"), 14 BCIT cohorts.

**Ranking conflict example:** at a website handover, Attuned says give the client creative control; Structured says the conventions must hold. Structured wins: the client edits *within* the conventions, using the guide. [ASSUMPTION: inferred from how Taylor describes the handover]

## Clusters considered (Step 3)

- **A (chosen): Structured · Crafted · Attuned,** with ownership as the essence. No alternative can claim "attuned," and it carries the teaching and mentoring Taylor loves.
- **B: Structured · Crafted · Owned.** Strongest match for hirers (6/6 posts ask for ownership), but differentiates less and loses the people side.
- **C: Clear · Crafted · Owned.** "Clear" nearly fails the Competitor Test.

## Words added to the board (Step 1b)

- **Recommenders:** sharp, dedicated, "took rough concepts and turned them into full products," owned (Dawson) · execution speed, adaptability, strategic clarity, aligned and structured, accessible terms, conventions, documentation, strategic technical partner (Bruno) · adaptable, quick study, hit the ground running, team mentality (Vaughn) · detail-oriented, wear different hats, in-depth explanation, break down a complex task, highest standards (Yogesh) · efficiency, depth, mentorship (Micheal).
- **Hirers' language:** end to end, shape the problem, product sensibility, polish, judgment, push back, builder-maker-designers, evidence.
- **Site and build buyers:** control, own, accountable, plain, live.
- **Learners:** anti-slop, track record.
- **The alternatives' own words (for the Competitor Test):** implement, throughput, full-stack, senior, years of experience · custom, seamless, scalable, leverage, hours · easy, templates, do it yourself · strategic, advisory, roadmap · 10x, unlock, AI-powered · transform, empower, journey.
