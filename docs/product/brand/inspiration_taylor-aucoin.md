### Observed on kaseyrandall.design (Claude's read, 14 Oct 2026; not Taylor's words)

Taylor asked Claude to "take a look at what subtleties are there." These are observations for thread 3 to weigh, not choices.

- **Opening:** a headshot, a casual greeting, a short self-aware intro, and a link into the full story.
- **Highlights:** a list of the roles he holds at once (design lead, game creator, founder and consultant, speaker, community builder, coach and mentor), with "See more" for the rest.
- **Section titles as identity statements:** Product Designer, Experienced, Storyteller, Community Builder, "Dreamer. Doer."
- **Work:** four featured projects. Each has an image, a one-line headline about the outcome for users, the company, a short paragraph and topic tags.
- **Testimonials:** six, each with a photo, full name, title and company.
- **Experience:** grouped as full-time, contract and fractional, then extras. Each entry has a logo, title, dates and location.
- **Proficiencies** as a plain list. Tools as icons under their own playful label.
- **Speaking, a podcast, communities and side projects** each get their own section.
- **Metrics attached to the thing they measure,** such as monthly visitors on a launch or member counts on a community.
- **A light/dark toggle** in the header.
