## How it goes

1. **You fill out the questionnaire.** Nine steps, about 45 minutes, longer if your back catalogue runs deep. Every question is optional and it saves as you type, so you can put it down and come back.
2. **You go through the taste gallery.** Real portfolio sites you can open and click around. You favourite the ones that catch you and say what caught you. This replaces the part where a designer shows you three drafts you don't like. It works like picking a template, except the thing it collects is why you picked it.
3. **You paste in whatever already exists.** Your filmography, your CV, your IMDb page, the projects off your old site. Paste the whole mess in, press a button, and it comes back sorted into entries you can correct.
4. **You record a voice note.** Two or three minutes on your phone: how you got into this, and one piece you're proud of and why. Don't script it. It's the most useful thing you can give me, because it's where the writing on the finished site gets its voice.
5. **I build it.** First look within three days of your final answers.
6. **You tell me what's wrong.** I send you a link to a real working site. Load it on your phone, open it in front of whoever's opinion you actually trust, and come back with the list.
7. **It goes live on your address.** You settle the balance and it goes live, usually the same day. Your existing email keeps working. That's part of the job.
8. **You get the keys.** Everything is already in your name by then, so handoff is a conversation rather than a transfer.
